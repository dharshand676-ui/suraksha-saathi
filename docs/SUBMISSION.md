**Project name:** Suraksha Saathi – Voice-first Vernacular Scam Shield
**Theme:** Digital Safety & Cyber Fraud Awareness

**One-line pitch:** Check any suspicious message in your own language, hear why it's a scam, file the complaint in one tap, and map scam hotspots for local awareness drives.

**Problem:** First-time digital users and senior citizens lose money to UPI fraud, fake KYC, digital-arrest threats and fake jobs. Awareness content is English, text-heavy and reactive.

**Solution:** A mobile-first web app + WhatsApp bot that (1) scores messages/links with explainable reasons in Hindi/English/Hinglish with voice in/out, (2) auto-drafts the complaint for cybercrime.gov.in / 1930, (3) aggregates reports into a scam map for panchayats, colleges and police, (4) teaches via a bilingual quiz run by MY Bharat volunteers.

**Innovation:** Explainable, offline-friendly detection tuned to Indian scams (digital arrest, UPI collect, KYC), plus a closed loop from detection to reporting to targeted awareness.

**Tech:** Python, Flask, SQLite, Web Speech API, Leaflet/OpenStreetMap, Twilio WhatsApp webhook, Docker.

**Impact metrics:** people reached, scams detected, complaints drafted, languages supported, quiz score improvement.

**Demo script (2 min):** 1) paste fake SBI KYC SMS → red verdict + Hindi voice. 2) tap Report → complaint letter. 3) open Scam map. 4) forward same message on WhatsApp. 5) volunteer quiz.
